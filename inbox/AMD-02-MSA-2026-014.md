# AMENDMENT NO. 2

**To:** Master Services Agreement MSA-2026-014
**Effective date:** 1 July 2026

Between **Northwind Supply Co.** and **Larkspur Media Ltd**.

The parties agree the following amendment to MSA-2026-014, effective from
1 July 2026. All other terms, including those introduced by Amendment No. 1,
remain unchanged.

## A. Clause 7 (Termination)

Clause 7 is amended so that either party may terminate on thirty (30) days
written notice. The immediate-termination provision for uncured material breach
is unchanged.

Signed for and on behalf of both parties on 26 June 2026.
