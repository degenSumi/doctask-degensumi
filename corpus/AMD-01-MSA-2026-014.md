# AMENDMENT NO. 1

**To:** Master Services Agreement MSA-2026-014
**Effective date:** 1 May 2026

Between **Northwind Supply Co.** and **Larkspur Media Ltd**.

The parties agree the following amendments to MSA-2026-014, effective from
1 May 2026. All other terms remain unchanged.

## A. Clause 3 (Fees)

Clause 3 is deleted and replaced with: "The Client shall pay the Supplier a
monthly fee of GBP 13,500, invoiced monthly in arrears."

## B. Clause 4 (Payment terms)

Clause 4 is amended so that payment terms are net forty-five (45) days from the
date of invoice. The late payment interest rate is unchanged.

## C. Clause 2 (Services)

The delivery window in clause 2 is reduced from thirty (30) days to fifteen (15)
days for orders marked urgent by the Client.

Signed for and on behalf of both parties on 24 April 2026.
