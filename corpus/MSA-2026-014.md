# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Reference:** MSA-2026-014
**Effective date:** 1 January 2026

Between **Northwind Supply Co.** ("the Supplier"), registered at 14 Harbour Row,
Bristol, and **Larkspur Media Ltd** ("the Client"), registered at 5 Ashfield
Gardens, Leeds.

## 1. Term

This agreement begins on 1 January 2026 and continues for twelve (12) months,
renewing annually unless either party gives notice under clause 7.

## 2. Services

The Supplier shall provide print production and fulfilment services as described
in Schedule A, and shall deliver all materials within thirty (30) days of a
written order.

## 3. Fees

The Client shall pay the Supplier a monthly fee of GBP 12,000, invoiced monthly
in arrears.

## 4. Payment terms

Payment terms are net thirty (30) days from the date of invoice. Late payment
accrues interest at 2% per month.

## 5. Purchase orders

Every invoice issued under this agreement must quote a valid purchase order
number supplied by the Client.

## 6. Data protection

The Supplier shall process personal data only on documented instructions from
the Client, and shall delete or return it on termination.

## 7. Termination

Either party may terminate on sixty (60) days written notice. The Client may
terminate immediately on material breach that remains uncured for fourteen (14)
days.

## 8. Governing law

This agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
